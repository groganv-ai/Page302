PAGE302 PRIVACY

Last updated: 16 October 2026

WHO RUNS PAGE302

Page302 is an independent football puzzle run by Vince Grogan Games. Page302 Reports is its issue-reporting feature. For privacy questions or requests, email vince.grogan.games@gmail.com.

REPORTS AND INQUIRIES

When you send a report, we receive its category and description, any club, season, player or manager name, suggested name or nickname you choose to provide, and your email address if supplied. The game version and game pack number are included automatically.

We use this information to investigate issues, improve the game and reply to inquiries. Your email is optional. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in a report.

Reports are delivered to the Vince Grogan Games inbox and are not published on the website. Report text and reply email addresses are not stored in the game’s database.

GMAIL ACCESS

Page302 Reports uses the Gmail API to send report emails through the operator’s authorised Google account. Players do not need to sign in to Google or grant access to their own Gmail accounts.

The integration requests permission to send email. It does not request permission to read Gmail messages, access contacts or browse the operator’s inbox. Google authorisation credentials are kept on the backend, outside the published website files.

Report contents and any supplied reply address are sent to Google for email delivery and storage. Gmail account data and report information are not sold or used for advertising or AI model training.

SCORES AND LEADERBOARDS

If you submit a score, we store your chosen initials, score, guess count, game pack, squad answers, submission time and a submission reference. Results may appear in the leaderboard and result lookup, including initials, scores and squad details. Use initials that you are comfortable showing publicly.

HOSTING AND ABUSE PREVENTION

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Cloudflare provides the backend and database, and Google provides Gmail delivery. These providers process technical information, such as IP addresses and request details, to operate and protect their services. The main game page also loads a font stylesheet from Google Fonts.

To limit repeated reports, the backend uses a daily-changing hash derived from an IP address rather than saving the raw address in report-limit counters. These counters expire after one to two days and are removed when a later report submission triggers cleanup. Hosting logs are separate from these counters.

KEEPING AND DELETING INFORMATION

Reports and replies are kept in the operator’s mailbox while needed to investigate issues and maintain a record of their resolution. Score records are kept to support game results and leaderboard history. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule for mailbox messages or score records.

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have submitted by emailing vince.grogan.games@gmail.com. Include enough detail to locate the report, or your game pack and score reference. We may need to confirm that a request relates to your information.

CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE

This page will be updated if Page302’s information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
