# Page302 style guide

- All visible button labels must be UPPERCASE, including labels created or changed by JavaScript. Examples: SCORE, RARITY, SEARCH, TOP 25. Preserve symbols such as arrows and close icons.
- Ranking buttons show the selected metric and direction. Use ↑ for HIGHEST FIRST and ↓ for LOWEST FIRST. Clearly distinguish the active button and expose its state to assistive technology.
- Preserve the established Teletext typography and Ceefax colour palette.

## Answer points colours

Colour the final awarded total, including linked-club bonuses. Do not change scoring.

| Colour | Player points | Manager points |
|---|---|---|
| White | 260 or below | Below 40 |
| Yellow | 261–519 | 40–59 |
| Green | 520–779 | 60–79 |
| Cyan | 780–1,039 | 80–99 |
| Magenta | 1,040+ | 100+ |
