PAGE302 HELP

OBJECTIVE

Can you build the rarest squad comprised of players from these 11 teams?

RULES

  • Players must have played at least one Premier League match during the specified season.
  • Complete the formation and manager (11 players + 1 manager).
  • Enter answers as:
    POS SURNAME CLUB
    e.g. AT SALAH LIV

PLAYER RARITY

UNI
= Unicorn
SUB
= Substitute
UTL
= Utility Player
ENG
= Engine Room
LEG
= Legend

CLUB BONUS

Receive a higher bonus the less you use each club.

Go 11 from 11 for the best score.

Managers provide additional bonus.

TIPS

  • 'Rarer' players score more points.
  • Players & Managers can represent more than one target club.
  • Experiment with different combinations to achieve a higher score.

ABOUT PAGE302

Hi, I'm Vince

Inspired by days spent watching football scores on Ceefax, my love for and experience in the videogame industry, and my useless knowledge of Premier League footballers, I created Page302.

It's a game I find fun and challenging, and I hope other people enjoy it too.
