PAGE302 HELP

OBJECTIVE

Can you build the rarest squad comprised of players from these 11 teams?

RULES

  • Players must have played at least one Premier League match during the specified season.
  • Complete the formation and manager (11 players + 1 manager).
  • Enter answers as:
    POS SURNAME CLUB
    e.g. AT SALAH LIV

PLAYER RARITY

UNI
= Unicorn
SUB
= Substitute
UTL
= Utility Player
ENG
= Engine Room
LEG
= Legend

CLUB BONUS

Receive a higher bonus the less you use each club.

Go 11 from 11 for the best score.

Managers provide additional bonus.

TIPS

  • 'Rarer' players score more points.
  • Players & Managers can represent more than one target club.
  • Experiment with different combinations to achieve a higher score.

ABOUT PAGE302

HI, I'M VINCE!

I've loved football and videogames for as long as I can remember. Some of my first jobs brought those interests together, working at Opta and testing Football Manager 2007.

And yes, I remember far too many old football players.

THE STORY SO FAR

I have spent much of my career making videogames, including LEGO titles and family games such as PAW Patrol World and Bluey: The Videogame.

Page302 has given me a chance to bring my experience and passions together in a game I think is fun and challenging. It's the sort of game I'd want to play, and I hope other people enjoy it too.
